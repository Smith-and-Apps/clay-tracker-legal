# Privacy Policy

**Clay Tracker**
**Effective Date: 12 March 2026**
**Developer: Robert Smith**

## Overview

Clay Tracker is a clay pigeon shooting performance tracking application. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Data Collection

Clay Tracker does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app operates entirely offline.

## Data Storage

All data you enter into Clay Tracker (shooting rounds, scores, venue preferences, gun details, and weather conditions) is stored **locally on your device only**. No data is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.

## Data You Control

You have full control over your data at all times:

- **Export**: You can export all your data as JSON or CSV files at any time via the Data Management menu.
- **Import**: You can import previously exported data back into the app.
- **Delete**: You can delete all your data at any time via the "Clear All Data" option.

## Analytics and Tracking

Clay Tracker does **not** use any analytics services, crash reporting tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. There are no cookies, no analytics, and no telemetry of any kind.

## Third-Party Services

Clay Tracker does **not** integrate with any third-party services. The app does not make any network requests.

## Permissions

Clay Tracker does not require any special device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or any other device features beyond basic app storage.

## Children's Privacy

Clay Tracker does not knowingly collect any data from children. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the app complies with applicable children's privacy regulations.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the revised version will be posted here with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
